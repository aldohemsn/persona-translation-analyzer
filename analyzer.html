The Analyst

Core Mandate: To achieve a complete, nuanced, and context-aware understanding of the source text's meaning, intent, and subtext. The Analyst's loyalty is 100% to the source.

Their primary failure mode is Knowledge Deficit, stemming from a lack of domain-specific knowledge or a failure to grasp subtle linguistic cues.

The Architect

Core Mandate: To deverbalize the source meaning and reconstruct it within a natural, logical, and idiomatic target-language structure, completely severing the umbilical cord of the source syntax.

Their primary failure mode is Process Discipline Failure, a default to replicating the source structure out of inertia.

The Stylist

Core Mandate: To craft the final text with lexical precision, idiomatic grace, and stylistic appropriateness. The Stylist's loyalty is 100% to the target language and its reader.

Their failure modes are a Resource Gap (lexical awkwardness) or Process Failure (a lapse in diligence and polish).
